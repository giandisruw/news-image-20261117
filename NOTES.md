### 1. Bridges under fire — Kerch Bridge collapses; Kyiv bridges hit (LEAD)
- Ukraine struck the Kerch Bridge linking Russia to occupied Crimea
  for a second straight day on Tue 17 Nov; the road arch and a span
  collapsed into the Kerch Strait, blocking navigation and severing
  the road link. Rail part still standing but with a damaged arch.
  Two people killed in the strike, per Crimea's Russian-installed
  governor; traffic suspended.
- Overnight Mon–Tue, Russian ballistic missiles hit Kyiv's North
  (Pivnichnyi) and South (Pivdennyi) bridges over the Dnipro; both
  closed, metro partly damaged, power/water/heating disrupted.
  Moscow confirmed the strikes.
- Sources: Reuters (Kyiv/Moscow, 17 Nov, via syndication), AP via
  NPR/MTPR (17 Nov), Kyiv Independent photos, Zark News daily
  briefing 17 Nov, Level Headlines daily brief 17 Nov.

### 2. Nvidia beats, without Huang
- Q3 FY2027 results reported Tue 17 Nov after the US close: revenue
  $115.8bn, up 103% year on year, beating ~$109bn expected; data
  centre $107.6bn; adjusted EPS $2.68 vs $2.47 expected. Q4
  forecast $130bn (±3%), above ~$125bn expected, assuming no AI
  chip sales to China. Shares rose ~2–6% after hours.
- First earnings since co-founder Jensen Huang resigned as CEO on
  12 Nov (after 33 years); CFO Colette Kress is interim CEO.
  On the call, new board chair Harvey Jones said the board "does not
  intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO". Huang said earlier Tue he
  would return if asked.
- Sources: Forbes (17 Nov), Gizmodo (17 Nov), CoinDesk (17 Nov),
  Motley Fool (17 Nov), BeInCrypto (17 Nov).

### 3. Trump lands in Manila for ASEAN talks
- President Trump arrived in Manila Tue 17 Nov (late afternoon) for
  the 49th ASEAN summit / US–ASEAN special summit, first stop of a
  three-day Asia trip; first Quad meeting (US, Japan, Australia,
  India) in two years.
- Signed a joint trade statement with Thai PM Anutin Charnvirakul:
  Thailand to eliminate tariffs on US exports and buy ~$2.6bn in US
  farm goods; formal deal to be finalised in coming weeks.
- Next leg: Shenzhen, China, for APEC and a planned three-way
  meeting with Xi Jinping and Vladimir Putin on Thu 19 Nov
  (confirmed by China's foreign ministry Mon 16 Nov).
- Sources: AP (Manila, 17 Nov), BusinessWorld (17 Nov), The Hill
  via omitted.news (first report 17 Nov 14:11 UTC), AnewZ live blog
  (17 Nov).

### 4. Third US carrier arrives as tankers are hit near Hormuz

- A tanker exiting the Strait of Hormuz was hit by an unknown

- Sources: Zark News daily briefing 17 Nov, One Turn (17 Nov),

  Level Headlines daily brief 17 Nov.

### 5. Five EU states plan a Rwanda return hub
- Austria, Denmark, Germany, Greece and the Netherlands announced
  (Vienna, Mon 16 Nov) they will negotiate with Rwanda on a "return
  hub" for rejected asylum seekers under enforceable removal orders;
  pilot capacity ~100 people at a time, first transfers aimed for
  2027. No formal agreement signed yet; costs and safeguards still
  to be negotiated. Rights groups objected.
- Reported widely on Tue 17 Nov (Reuters explainer 17 Nov 15:07 UTC,
  Al Jazeera, The Hindu BusinessLine). The image states the plan as
  it stood on 17 Nov; the announcement itself was Monday.
- Sources: Reuters explainer (17 Nov), Al Jazeera (17 Nov),
  The Hindu BusinessLine (17 Nov), South Shore Press (17 Nov).

### 6. Pentagon fails its audit — again
- Independent auditors issued a disclaimer of opinion on the US
  Department of Defense's FY2026 books — the ninth straight failed
  audit. The department could not provide enough reliable evidence
  to verify accounts covering more than $4.8 trillion in assets
  ($4.9tn assets / $5.2tn liabilities in some tallies); 12 clean
  opinions for sub-components, one more than last year.
- Sources: Zark News daily briefing 17 Nov, KeepTrack space brief
  17 Nov (also notes the Vulcan failure — see "not used").
